## Missing lecture coverage

**Q027 — controller disconnection behavior.** [Lesson 14](14_controller_responses.md) retains rule timeouts, [lesson 15](15_reactive_handshake.md) explains local forwarding with installed valid entries, and [lesson 19](19_systems_challenges.md) lists controller reliability. The guide does not explain which forwarding mode a switch enters when all controller connections fail. Targeted searches of networking guides 01–05 found no such explanation. The source question's likely stale-rule outcome therefore needs missing mechanism and a stated configuration. Its primary lesson remains empty and dependencies unresolved; it is not silently assigned to the general reliability bullet.

The supplementary OpenFlow check below identifies the missing distinction without turning it into new lecture teaching. Missing coverage does not establish that this topic is outside the exam.
